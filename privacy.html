Privacy notice

Last updated 27 November 2026

Who I am

I am Tina, and I run Tina Talks. I decide how your details are used, which makes me the data controller. You can reach me at hello@tinatalks.uk.

What I collect

  • Your first name, last name, email address and mobile number.
  • Your answers to the quiz, including anything you choose to tell me about your faith, your health, your work and your finances.
  • When you started and finished the quiz, and which questions you reached.

Why I use it

I read your answers myself and send you a personal plan, usually within 48 hours. If it looks like a good fit, I may also tell you about my coaching. I use your details to contact you about your plan and nothing else.

My legal basis

I use your details because you asked for your plan and because you ticked the box to agree. Some of what you may share, such as your faith or your health, is more sensitive. I only use that with your clear consent, which is the tick box on the details page. You can withdraw it at any time.

Where it is kept and who sees it

  • Your answers are saved in a private Google Sheet that only I can open, using Google services. Google may process data outside the UK, with safeguards in place.
  • I do not sell your details or share them for advertising.
  • The quiz page is hosted on GitHub, and it loads fonts from Google. Those services can see your IP address when the page loads.
  • The quiz does not use tracking or advertising cookies.

How long I keep it

I keep your details for up to 24 months after we last spoke, then delete them. If you ask me to delete them sooner, I will.

Your rights

You can ask me to show you what I hold about you, correct it, delete it, or stop using it. You can also withdraw your consent whenever you like. Email hello@tinatalks.uk and I will reply within one month.

If something goes wrong

If you are unhappy with how I have handled your details, please tell me first. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.
